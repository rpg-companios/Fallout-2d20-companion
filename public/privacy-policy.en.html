Privacy Policy

«Positronium» — last updated: August 26, 2026.

This app is a companion tool for the Fallout 2d20 tabletop role-playing game. We respect your privacy and follow a minimal-data principle. This policy describes what data is processed and how.

1. Data we collect

The app stores and syncs only the data you create yourself in the game: character name, attributes, skills, perks, equipment, inventory and character settings. These are character files in the rpg-companion-character format.

We do not collect or process:

  • personal data (full name, phone number, email address, home address);
  • location data;
  • payment information or bank card details;
  • browsing history or sites visited.

2. Cloud sync (Google Drive)

The “Sync to Google Drive” feature keeps copies of your characters in the cloud so you can restore them on other devices. For this the app uses the app-only folder (appDataFolder) in your Google Drive.

  • Character files are stored in this hidden folder and are not visible in your normal Google Drive interface.
  • Only the app itself has access to this folder.
  • You can disable sync or delete your files at any time; if you remove the app or revoke Drive access, these copies stop updating.

The files themselves are game data. We do not read or analyze their content, do not share them with third parties, and do not use them for advertising.

3. Google sign-in

Sync uses standard OAuth 2.0 sign-in with your Google account. The app only obtains access needed for the feature to work: to your app-only folder in Google Drive. We do not need access to your email, contacts, calendar or other personal information.

4. Local storage

The app stores character data locally in your browser/device (app local storage). It stays on your device and is not sent to our servers, since the app has no dedicated server-side store.

5. Cookies and analytics

The app does not use third-party trackers, ad networks or web-analytics tools. We do not collect anonymized usage statistics.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. The only external service the app interacts with is Google Drive (for cloud sync, at your explicit request).

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data. However, data transfer over the internet and local storage cannot be absolutely secure. Keep access to your Google account and device safe.

8. Your rights

You can stop using cloud sync at any time. Your rights over local data and your Google Drive link are under your control: disable sync in the app or revoke app access in your Google account security settings.
